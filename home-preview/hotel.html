The Heart of Khan Wahoud

The Courtyard

For nearly three centuries, life at the Khan has turned around its courtyard. Merchants arrived here. Travellers rested here. Cultures, trades and stories crossed paths beneath its arches. Today, it gathers Damascus once again.

Why This Place Matters

Built in 1736. Alive again today.

Khan Suleiman Pasha has stood at the heart of historic Damascus for centuries — a caravanserai where trade, travel and culture met. Its story did not end. It began a new chapter.
